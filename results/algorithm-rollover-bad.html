Algorithm rollover RSA (8) to ECDSA (13), rushed

Zone bank.test. · conservative model · propagation 5m · registry delay 1h · max cache TTL 7d · start 2026-11-02 12:00 UTC
UNSAFESERVFAIL for up to 22h 7m

New ECDSA keys, new signatures and the new DS are all introduced at once, and the RSA keys are removed three hours later. Resolvers that cached the old DNSKEY RRset or the old DS RRset see a broken chain of trust.

What breaks

T+1h (Mon 02 Nov 13:00 UTC) → T+1h 5m (Mon 02 Nov 13:05 UTC) · 5m
Resolvers may still hold DNSKEY v0 in their cache until T+1h 5m (Mon 02 Nov 13:05 UTC), but from T+1h (Mon 02 Nov 13:00 UTC) they validate it against DS v1 (step "Add ECDSA keys and signatures, submit the new DS"). DNSKEY becomes bogus: no chain of trust from DS to DNSKEY: none of the keys the DS RRset points to {KSK13} is in the DNSKEY RRset {KSK8, ZSK8}. Resolvers can keep answering SERVFAIL for up to 1m after that.
T+3h (Mon 02 Nov 15:00 UTC) → T+1d 1h (Tue 03 Nov 13:00 UTC) · 22h
From T+3h (Mon 02 Nov 15:00 UTC) the zone serves DNSKEY v2 (step "Remove the RSA keys and signatures"), but resolvers may still hold DS v0 in their cache until T+1d 1h (Tue 03 Nov 13:00 UTC). DNSKEY becomes bogus: no chain of trust from DS to DNSKEY: none of the keys the DS RRset points to {KSK8} is in the DNSKEY RRset {KSK13, ZSK13}. Resolvers can keep answering SERVFAIL for up to 1m after that.

Resolver profile: worst. A failed chain of trust is remembered for 1m and a failed answer for 1m. The zone itself cannot be validated for 22h 5m.

Safe schedule

  • Split "Add ECDSA keys and signatures, submit the new DS": the change to DS must wait until the old data has expired from caches, so it moves to a new step 1h 5m later

Notes

  • info · plan: DS changes take effect 1h after submission (registry delay)
Worst-case analysis: a version can stay in a resolver's cache for a full TTL after the last authoritative server stopped serving it. Conservative model = RFC 7583 assumptions (any cached versions may be combined, as for validators behind caches); fetch model = a validating resolver that validates on fetch and trusts its cache.
