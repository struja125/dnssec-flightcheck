What breaks

T+30s → T+2m 5s · 1m 35s
From T+30s the zone serves DNSKEY v1 (step "Swap KSK1 for KSK2 in the DNSKEY RRset"), but resolvers may still hold DS v0 in their cache until T+2m 5s. DNSKEY becomes bogus: no chain of trust from DS to DNSKEY: none of the keys the DS RRset points to {KSK1} is in the DNSKEY RRset {KSK2, ZSK1}. Resolvers can keep answering SERVFAIL for up to 1m after that.

Resolver profile: worst. A failed chain of trust is remembered for 1m and a failed answer for 1m. The zone itself cannot be validated for 1m 35s.

Notes

  • info · plan: DS changes take effect 5s after submission (registry delay)
Worst-case analysis: a version can stay in a resolver's cache for a full TTL after the last authoritative server stopped serving it. Conservative model = RFC 7583 assumptions (any cached versions may be combined, as for validators behind caches); fetch model = a validating resolver that validates on fetch and trusts its cache.
