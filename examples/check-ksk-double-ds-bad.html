DNSSEC FlightCheck

KSK rollover (double-DS), KSK swapped too early

Zone bank.test. · conservative model · propagation 5m · registry delay 1h · max cache TTL 7d · start 2026-11-02 12:00 UTC
UNSAFESERVFAIL for up to 23h 1m

The operator submits the DS for the new KSK and swaps the KSK in the DNSKEY RRset two hours later. Resolvers that cached the old DS RRset (TTL 1 day) cannot validate the new DNSKEY RRset.

What breaks

T+2h (Mon 02 Nov 14:00 UTC) → T+1d 1h (Tue 03 Nov 13:00 UTC) · 23h
From T+2h (Mon 02 Nov 14:00 UTC) the zone serves DNSKEY v1 (step "Swap KSK1 for KSK2 in the DNSKEY RRset"), but resolvers may still hold DS v0 in their cache until T+1d 1h (Tue 03 Nov 13:00 UTC). DNSKEY becomes bogus: no chain of trust from DS to DNSKEY: none of the keys the DS RRset points to {KSK1} is in the DNSKEY RRset {KSK2, ZSK1}. Resolvers can keep answering SERVFAIL for up to 1m after that.

Resolver profile: worst. A failed chain of trust is remembered for 1m and a failed answer for 1m. The zone itself cannot be validated for 23h.

Notes

  • info · plan: DS changes take effect 1h after submission (registry delay)
Worst-case analysis: a version can stay in a resolver's cache for a full TTL after the last authoritative server stopped serving it. Conservative model = RFC 7583 assumptions (any cached versions may be combined, as for validators behind caches); fetch model = a validating resolver that validates on fetch and trusts its cache.
